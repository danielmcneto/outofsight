# Out of Sight 

 **Work in Progress:** *Out of Sight* is an 8-week prototype project inspired by PSX stealth classics. Features, mechanics, and assets are subject to change.

---

## The Game

**Out of Sight** is an upcoming game built with ***Unity***. 

**Out of Sight is a stealth-action game inspired by the PSX classic Metal Gear Solid.**

*You play as a rogue mercenary navigating a bleak, dystopian world ruled by an all-seeing data conglomerate. In this corrupt society, information is the ultimate weapon, the company controls the global narrative, deciding who rises to power and who getsforgotten.*

Everything changes when you take on an **impossible contract**: launch a secret attack from within and bring down the empire.
